Services

What we examine, and how we report it.

Each service ends in a signed report tied to a verifiable number. Choose a service below, or ask us which report fits your stone.

01

Gemstone identification

Species and variety confirmed by refractive index, spectroscopy and microscopy — natural, synthetic or imitation, stated without ambiguity.

02

Certification & reports

A full report recording weight, dimensions, colour, cut and any observations, issued under a unique verifiable number.

03

Diamond grading

Colour, clarity, cut and carat assessed against consistent laboratory standards, with plotting where relevant.

04

Origin determination

For rubies, sapphires and emeralds, a geographic origin opinion based on inclusion study and trace-element analysis.

05

Treatment detection

Heat, fracture-filling, diffusion and other enhancements identified and disclosed clearly on the report.

06

Jewellery appraisal

Examination of mounted stones and finished pieces, describing each component as set.

Not sure which report you need?

Tell us about the stone and its intended use — sale, insurance, or your own records — and we will point you to the right examination.
