What we do

One laboratory, three roles.

We are a testing laboratory first — identification, treatment detection and origin determination carried out on calibrated instruments by qualified gemologists.

We are a source of trust for the trade — jewellers, auction houses and collectors rely on an SGTL report to describe a stone without exaggeration.

And we are a verification service — anyone holding a stone can check its report number against our records and confirm it is genuine.

Every report carries a number. Every number can be checked.

Enter a report number and confirm the grading, weight and identification recorded by the laboratory — no login, no cost.
