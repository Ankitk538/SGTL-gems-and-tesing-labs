About

Independence is the whole point.

A grading report is only worth the neutrality behind it. SGTL has no stake in the sale of any stone it examines — our finding is the same whether it helps a price or hurts it.

Method

Examined, not estimated.

Every stone is handled by a qualified gemologist and tested on calibrated instruments. Where a finding depends on interpretation — an origin opinion, a treatment call — we say so, and we describe what we observed rather than what we assume.

We follow established gemological nomenclature so that a term on our report means the same thing it means across the trade.

Trust

A report you can check.

Each report is issued under a unique number recorded in our database. Anyone holding the stone can enter that number on our verification page and confirm the grading came from this laboratory — the simplest defence against altered or counterfeit paperwork.
