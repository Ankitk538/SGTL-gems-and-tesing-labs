Verify report
// test suite

Six examinations, one signed report.

Each service is a defined procedure with a defined output. Every one ends in a report tied to a verifiable number.

SVC 01

Gemstone identification

Species and variety by RI, spectroscopy and microscopy. Natural / synthetic / imitation stated without ambiguity.

SVC 02

Certification & reports

Full report: weight, dimensions, colour, cut and observations, under a unique verifiable number.

SVC 03

Diamond grading

Colour, clarity, cut and carat against consistent laboratory standards, with plotting where relevant.

SVC 04

Origin determination

Geographic origin opinion for ruby, sapphire and emerald from inclusion and trace-element study.

SVC 05

Treatment detection

Heat, fracture-filling, diffusion and other enhancements identified and disclosed on the report.

SVC 06

Jewellery appraisal

Examination of mounted stones and finished pieces, each component described as set.
