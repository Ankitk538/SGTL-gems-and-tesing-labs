// about the laboratory

Neutral by design.

A grading report is worth exactly as much as the independence behind it. SGTL does not buy, sell or broker the stones it tests.

// principles

How we keep a report honest.

01

Examined, not estimated

Every stone is handled by a qualified gemologist on calibrated instruments. Interpretive findings are labelled as opinions.

02

Consistent nomenclature

We follow established gemological terminology so a term on our report means the same across the trade.

03

Traceable numbering

Each report is issued under a unique number recorded in our database and checkable by anyone.

04

Disclosure first

Treatments and enhancements are stated plainly, even when disclosure lowers a stone's desirability.
