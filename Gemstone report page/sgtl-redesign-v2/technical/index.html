// how a stone is reported

The chain from bench to report number.

STEP 01

Intake & weighing

Weight and dimensions recorded on calibrated scales; the stone is logged under a unique report number.

STEP 02

Identification

Refractive index, spectroscopy and microscopy confirm species and variety — natural, synthetic or imitation.

STEP 03

Treatment & origin

Inclusion study and trace-element analysis detect enhancement and, where applicable, inform an origin opinion.

STEP 04

Report & verification

Findings are signed off and published to the database so the number can be checked by anyone holding the stone.
